These cars ensure everyone arrives together and on time.

The Journey Home

At the end of the celebrations, it is traditional for the bride and groom to travel together, marking their first journey as a married couple. Whether it is in a luxurious Rolls Royce or a vintage car, this special ride is the perfect way to close out the day, offering a quiet and romantic moment after a long and joyous celebration.

When it comes to wedding car hire, traditional and modern trends often guide how costs are split. A common approach involves both the bride and groom taking responsibility for their respective transport needs, with some subtle differences in how the services are used.

Traditional Roles in Covering Wedding Cars

Traditionally, the groom typically pays for his own wedding car, while the bride or her family covers the cost of her car. However, the way the cars are used differs:

The Bride’s Car: Brides often hire a vintage wedding car as a drop-off service, ensuring they arrive in style for the ceremony and, if needed, to the reception. This keeps costs lower as the car is only needed for a shorter duration.
‍
The Groom’s Car: Grooms tend to hire a car for 3-6 hours, not only to get to the venue but also to provide a return journey for the couple after the wedding. This transport is typically used to whisk the newlyweds away to a lush hotel or their home at the end of the day.
